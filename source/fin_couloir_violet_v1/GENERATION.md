# FVC1 — génération et sources

Source visuelle : `large.S05P03A.png.301f7a1eadda348357be0801e81faa2a.png` (référence réutilisée par ECV1). Les images complètes et leurs empreintes sont reprises dans `renders/fin_couloir_violet_v1/manifest.json`.

## Décor (`bruts/decor.png`)

Prompt envoyé au générateur avec la capture comme référence :

> Create a new wide 4:3 top-down end-room map in EXACTLY the same Nintendo DS Pokémon Mystery Dungeon Explorers of Sky pixel-art style, palette, pixel cluster size and rock/floor texture as the supplied reference screenshot. Treat the reference as the canonical asset guide; do not invent a different visual style. Dark indigo-violet stacked boulder walls, muted mauve-gray textured stone floor, small pebble clusters only. Layout: the player enters from the SOUTH through one narrow straight rocky corridor, which opens continuously into a broad irregular oval boss chamber in the upper two-thirds. The chamber floor is wide, empty and playable; a naturally uneven ring of the same stacked purple boulders frames the chamber and the map edges. A small scattering of the same tiny gray-violet pebbles along the perimeter, but clear route from the south entrance through the chamber to the north-center objective marker area. Keep the exact subdued blue-violet hues, low contrast, discrete hard-edged 1x/2x pixel clusters and compact repeating boulder forms visible in the reference. This is a PMD dungeon screenshot, not modern painted concept art: no antialiasing, no airbrush, no glossy gradients, no 3D. No water, no waterfall, no lava, no plants, no flowers, no butterflies, no crystals, no fruit, no shrine, no altar, no cave door, no glow, no torches, no characters, no boss sprite, no signage, no text, no UI, no border or grid. The objective is only an open patch of floor at north center; do not add an object there.

## Segmentation (`bruts/objets_magenta.png`)

Generated as an edit of `decor.png`: replace only the playable stone floor—including the southern corridor—with flat RGB `#FF00FF`; preserve walls, pebbles and dark boundary. The mask is used only by the builder and never exported. An exported-color test guards against magenta leakage.

## Underpainting (`bruts/sol_complet.png`)

Not generated by the image model: created reproducibly by `build.py` by tiling a 340×260 px clean floor sample from `decor.png` across the canvas. Non-floor pixels in the sample (if any) are replaced by the nearest valid floor pixel. The separated opaque floor, shadows, walls, stones and void cover it in the final composition. This is an editable substrate, not a claim of an original game texture.

## Animation

The pebble sprites are the already extracted 7×6, 10×7 and 12×12 px ECV1 poses. Pixel colors and sizes are retained. FVC1 computes new falling paths; this movement is not a PMD ROM animation.

## `bruts/decor_magenta.png`

> WIDE LANDSCAPE 4:3, zoomed out. Design one original overhead PMD-style map called Lac de Verre: an underground frozen lake inside an ancient blue-grey ice cavern. Use reference 1 (Red PMDO Whiscash Pond, T01P02A) only for PMD water-edge, shore and path material language; use reference 2 (Sky PMDO D52P11A) only for cold cave, ice and crystal textures. Do not copy either map's layout and do not use a waterfall-cave composition. Create a new symmetric-but-natural layout: a continuous pale stone/frost path from the south entrance to a small arch at the north, with a broad central lake, stepped icy ledges, a few isolated ice crystals and snow banks along the edges. Keep the playable path clearly connected from south to north. This is the DECOR plate for magenta-key segmentation: fill the entire lake and every small water pool with perfectly flat opaque pure magenta RGB #FF00FF; fill the outside-of-map void with the exact same pure magenta. Use pure magenta nowhere else; no water texture, reflections, foam or shadows. All ice, snow, stone and crystals must remain inside a clear land silhouette. No tropical trees or green jungle, no text, UI, characters, creatures, icons, border, particles, blur, antialiasing or collage. Crisp PMD pixel-art forms, cold blue-grey / icy cyan palette guided by the references. Keep the complete map fully in frame with margins. Target 4:3 landscape, approximately 1200 by 896 pixels.
